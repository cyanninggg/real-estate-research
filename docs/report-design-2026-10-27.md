# Report presentation — 27 October 2026

## Design

The homepage presents professional focus, capabilities, the featured Hew research, Explore Research and View Resume in the opening screen. Two numbered cases link to their respective research. The shared palette uses deep navy, bright blue and white with restrained dividers; no reference-report photos, logos or pages are reproduced.

Hew follows insight → evidence → technical detail. Its six sections remain intact. New presentation components include a three-finding executive summary, direct-label lease chart, public-tax exhibit, separate illustrative financial flow, sensitivity matrix, source-to-model trace panels and finding / implication / action rows. Existing exact-value tables, sources and limitations remain accessible. The financial flow's blocks explain sequence rather than magnitude. Sensitivity colors compare each row with its supplied base case; they do not define an investment threshold.

## Research boundaries

- Public property tax is a real-property benchmark, not a replacement for hypothetical model tax.
- Lease-level proceeds after free rent and broker assumptions are not NOI.
- The development model is illustrative; its value is not Hew's actual valuation.
- Broker fees and replacement reserves remain below NOI.
- Charts read existing curated data; no model assumptions or financial results changed.
- The sanitized public workbook is separate from the private source workbook.

## Accessibility and delivery

System fonts and HTML/CSS exhibits avoid new libraries. Figures include exact numeric labels, units, periods and source notes. Technical tables remain keyboard-focusable and scroll within their containers on small screens. Section navigation has a mobile selector. Reduced-motion settings are respected. The standalone brief provides Letter print styles without navigation or interactive controls in the print layout.

The site remains buildless and uses the existing GitHub Pages branch deployment. No backend, tracking service, deployment workflow or credentials were added.
