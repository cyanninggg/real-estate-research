Analytical framework

Market evidence → Effective rent → Operating costs → NOI → Sensitivity → Decision implications

Compare selected lease examples; reconcile municipal tax fields; test a separate development scenario one input at a time. Broker costs and replacement reserves remain below NOI.

Implications for underwriting

  • Match lease dates, layouts and signed terms before interpreting a rental premium.
  • Verify expense responsibility and the actual tax bill before relying on an NOI estimate.
  • Use the model to identify diligence needs, not to establish an acquisition price.

Sources and limitations

Sources: historical leasing observations (Market Evidence!A5:H7); City of Boston Assessing, FY2026 parcel 2101831000, checked 26 October 2026; curated public underwriting model, Pro Forma and Sensitivity sheets. Exact municipal links, formulas and evidence notes are available in the full research report.

Selected observations are not a rent roll or confirmed executed leases. Public tax evidence is not substituted into the model. The model represents one stabilized year, with assumed operating costs and no subsidy, tax relief or permanent debt. Investment diligence still requires a rent roll, T-12, signed terms, capital plan, operating bills, tax payment records and financing.
